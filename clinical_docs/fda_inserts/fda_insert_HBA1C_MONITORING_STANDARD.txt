PART I – WHAT IS HbA1c?
─────────────────────────
FULL NAME      : Hemoglobin A1c (Glycated Hemoglobin)
LOINC CODE     : 4548-4
DESCRIPTION    :
  HbA1c measures the percentage of hemoglobin in red blood cells that has become
  glycated (bonded with glucose). Because red blood cells survive approximately
  8–12 weeks, HbA1c reflects average blood glucose levels over a 2–3 month
  period. It is the gold standard for long-term glycemic assessment in diabetes
  management.

REFERENCE RANGES:
  < 5.7%       : Normal (non-diabetic)
  5.7% – 6.4%  : Prediabetes
  ≥ 6.5%       : Diabetes diagnosis (if confirmed by repeat testing)
  < 7.0%       : ADA target for most non-pregnant adults with T2DM
  < 8.0%       : Acceptable target for select populations (elderly, high
                  hypoglycemia risk, limited life expectancy, multiple comorbidities)
  ≥ 9.0%       : Poor glycemic control – intensification of therapy required

PART II – HEDIS MEASURE: COMPREHENSIVE DIABETES CARE (CDC)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
MEASURE ID     : HEDIS CDC – HbA1c Testing
NQF ID         : NQF 0059
DESCRIPTION    : The percentage of members 18–75 years of age with type 2 diabetes
                 mellitus who had a HbA1c test during the measurement year.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
  - Age 18–75 as of December 31 of the measurement year
  - Enrolled in the health plan for at least 11 months
  - Active diagnosis of Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus (ICD-10: E11.x)
  - Excludes: patients with Type 1 DM; gestational DM; patients in hospice

NUMERATOR:
  At least ONE HbA1c test (LOINC 4548-4 or equivalent) performed and resulted
  during the measurement year (January 1 – December 31).

DENOMINATOR:
  All eligible members (see criteria above).

PERFORMANCE LEVELS (NCQA National Benchmarks 2025):
  ≥ 5th percentile   : < 71% compliance
  50th percentile     : ~82% compliance
  90th percentile     : ~90% compliance
  Highest performing  : ~92%+

HEDIS RELATED SUB-MEASURES (same CDC domain):
  - HbA1c Poor Control (> 9.0%) – lower rate is better
  - HbA1c Control < 8.0%        – higher rate is better
  - HbA1c Control < 7.0%        – higher rate is better
  - BP Control < 130/80          – higher rate is better
  - Eye exam (retinal screening)
  - Statin therapy prescribed
  - Nephropathy monitoring (urine microalbumin or ACE/ARB use)

PART III – ADA STANDARDS OF CARE: HbA1c TESTING FREQUENCY
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Source: ADA Standards of Medical Care in Diabetes – 2026

Section 6 – Glycemic Targets:
"Perform HbA1c testing at least two times a year in patients who are meeting
treatment goals and who have stable glycemic control."

"Perform HbA1c testing quarterly in patients whose therapy has changed or
who are not meeting glycemic goals."

"HbA1c goals should be individualized based on:
  - Duration of diabetes
  - Age and life expectancy
  - Comorbid conditions (especially cardiovascular and renal disease)
  - Hypoglycemia unawareness or severe hypoglycemia history
  - Individual patient preferences and resources"

Key Point (ADA 2026):
"A reasonable HbA1c goal for many non-pregnant adults is < 7.0%. Less
stringent goals (such as < 8.0%) may be appropriate for patients with a
history of severe hypoglycemia, limited life expectancy, advanced microvascular
or macrovascular complications, or extensive comorbid conditions."
